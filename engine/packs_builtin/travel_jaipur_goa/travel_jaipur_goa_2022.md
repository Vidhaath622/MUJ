# Travel Guide: Jaipur & Goa (2022 Archive Edition)

## Jaipur Sights & Entry Fees (2022)
- City Palace Composite Ticket: A combined entrance ticket costs ₹400 for Indian adults, which includes 1-week entry access to Jaigarh Fort.
- Hawa Mahal Entry Fee: Standard admission is ₹50 for domestic visitors and ₹200 for international tourists. Entry is free on 18th May (International Museum Day).
- Amber Fort Light & Sound Show: The daily Hindi light show ticket costs ₹200 per person at the venue.
- Albert Hall Museum Timings: Open daily from 9:00 AM to 5:00 PM for day visits, and 7:00 PM to 10:00 PM for night viewing.

## Goa Local Transport & Attractions (2022)
- Mandovi River Evening Cruise: Public 1-hour sunset cruise organized by GTDC costs ₹300 per passenger.
- Dudhsagar Waterfalls Jeep Safari: Mandatory jeep trek operator charges ₹500 per seat from the Collem junction.
- Pilar Monastery Museum Entry: The entry ticket for the historical artifact collection is ₹20 per visitor.
