# General Tourist Guidelines for Rajasthan and Goa

## Photography & Conduct
- Camera Fees: Professional DSLR camera permits at state-protected heritage sites cost ₹100 per device unless waived.
- Monuments Maintenance Notice: Drone flying is strictly prohibited over all heritage forts and sanctuaries without explicit Archeological Survey of India (ASI) clearance.
- Beach Safety Regulations: Swimming at Goa beaches after 6:00 PM is strictly prohibited due to strong ocean currents.
