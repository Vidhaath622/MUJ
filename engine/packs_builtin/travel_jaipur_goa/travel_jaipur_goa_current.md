# Travel Guide: Jaipur & Goa (Current Edition)

## Jaipur Sights & Entry Fees (Current)
- City Palace Composite Ticket: Composite entrance ticket costs ₹700 for Indian adults, which includes same-day admission to Jaigarh Fort.
- Hawa Mahal Entry Fee: Standard admission is ₹100 for domestic visitors and ₹500 for international tourists. Entry remains free on 18th May (International Museum Day).
- Amber Fort Light & Sound Show: The daily Hindi light show ticket costs ₹350 per person at the venue.
- Albert Hall Museum Timings: Open daily from 9:00 AM to 7:00 PM continuously, with no separate night show closure.

## Goa Local Transport & Attractions (Current)
- Mandovi River Evening Cruise: Public 1-hour sunset cruise organized by GTDC costs ₹500 per passenger.
- Dudhsagar Waterfalls Jeep Safari: Mandatory jeep trek operator charges ₹800 per seat from the Collem junction.
- Pilar Monastery Museum Entry: The entry ticket for the historical artifact collection is ₹50 per visitor.
