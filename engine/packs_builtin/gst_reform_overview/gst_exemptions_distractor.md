# GST Exempted Items and Special Rates Guidelines

Certain items and essential service sectors maintain full exemption status or separate tax treatment outside standard rationalisation slabs.

## Exempted Goods and Services
- Healthcare Services: Services provided by recognized doctors, hospitals, and medical clinics remain fully exempt from GST.
- Fresh Unprocessed Food: Fresh fruits, fresh vegetables, unbranded milk, and curd carry 0% GST.
- Educational Services: Core educational services provided by schools and recognized universities are exempt from GST.
