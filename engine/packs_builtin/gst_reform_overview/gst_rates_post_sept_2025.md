# GST Rate Schedule (Post September 2025 Rationalisation)

Effective 22nd September 2025, the GST Council enacted a landmark rate rationalisation overhaul, consolidating the tax structure primarily into two main slabs of 5% and 18%.

## Revised Rates for Selected Common Items (Post-Reform)
- Air Conditioners and TVs (>32 inches): Consumer durables including ACs and large LED/LCD TVs reduced from 28% to 18%.
- Cement and Construction Materials: GST on cement reduced from 28% to 18% to boost housing and infrastructure.
- Medicines and Medical Devices: 33 life-saving drugs and diagnostic kits reduced to 0% (Nil), with other standard medicines reduced from 12% to 5%.
- Spectacles and Corrective Lenses: GST on spectacles, lenses, and contact lenses reduced from 12% to 5%.
- Hotel Room Accommodation: Hotel stays up to ₹7,500 per day reduced from 12% to 5%.
- Gyms and Salons: Services provided by gyms, salons, barbers, and yoga centers reduced from 18% to 5%.
- Packaged Processed Foods: Packaged food items including pasta, namkeens, sauces, and biscuits reduced to 5%.
- Small Automobiles & Two-Wheelers: Small cars and two-wheelers (≤350cc) reduced from 28% to 18%.
- Bicycles and Toothpaste: Household articles like soaps, toothbrushes, toothpaste, and bicycles reduced to 5%.
