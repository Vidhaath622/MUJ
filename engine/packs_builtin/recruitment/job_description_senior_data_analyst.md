# Job Description: Senior Data Analyst

Fictional demo document. Northwind Talent Co. is a made-up company used for testing.

Team: Customer Insights. Reports to: Head of Analytics. Requisition number: NW-DA-2026-014.

## About the role

The Senior Data Analyst builds dashboards, designs A/B test analyses and explains customer trends to product and marketing leaders. The role owns the weekly retention report.

## Requirements

Candidates need a minimum of 3 years of relevant experience, in line with the current hiring policy. Strong SQL and experience with at least one BI tool such as Power BI or Tableau are required.

Python or R is preferred but not required. A degree in statistics, economics, computer science or a related field is expected.

## Compensation

The salary band for this role is $62,000 to $78,000 per year. Offers above $78,000 need sign-off from the regional finance lead.

Candidates who relocate to Pune for this role receive a one-time joining bonus of $4,000, paid with their first salary.

## Location and hybrid rules

The role is based in the Pune office. Employees in this role must work from the Pune office at least 3 days per week, and Tuesday is a mandatory office day for the whole team.

Fully remote work is not available for this role. Short remote periods of up to 2 weeks per year need written approval from the Head of Analytics.

## Interview stages

The interview process has 4 stages: a recruiter screen, a SQL take-home assignment, a case study panel and a final interview with the Head of Analytics.

The SQL take-home assignment has a time limit of 48 hours from when it is sent. The case study panel lasts 60 minutes and includes two analysts from the Customer Insights team.

Candidates who do not pass the take-home assignment cannot reapply for the same role for 6 months.

## Equipment and onboarding

New Senior Data Analysts receive a laptop and access to the analytics warehouse on their first day. Onboarding lasts 2 weeks and includes a data governance course that must be passed before production data access is granted.

Each new hire is paired with a buddy from the Customer Insights team for their first 3 months.
