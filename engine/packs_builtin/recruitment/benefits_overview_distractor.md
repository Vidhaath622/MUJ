# Northwind Talent Co. Employee Benefits and Relocation Overview

Fictional demo document. Northwind Talent Co. is a made-up company used for testing.

This overview describes benefits for current employees. It does not cover hiring, offers or recruitment.

## Annual bonus

Permanent employees are eligible for an annual performance bonus of up to 12% of base salary. The bonus is paid in April each year for the previous financial year.

Employees who joined less than 3 months before the end of the financial year are not eligible for that year's bonus.

## Relocation for current employees

Current employees who transfer between the Pune and Jaipur offices receive a relocation allowance of $6,000. The relocation allowance must be claimed within 90 days of the transfer date.

Employees who leave Northwind within 12 months of a relocation must repay 50% of the relocation allowance.

## Leave and years of service

Employees receive 20 days of paid annual leave. Employees with 5 years of service receive 25 days of paid annual leave, and employees with 10 years of service receive 30 days.

Unused leave of up to 10 days can be carried over to the next year.

## Resignation notice period

Employees who resign must serve a notice period of 30 days. Senior employees must serve a notice period of 90 days.

Notice periods can be shortened by mutual agreement with the manager and HR.

## Health and wellness

All employees are covered by group health insurance from their first day. The wellness allowance is $500 per year and can be used for gym memberships or fitness classes.

Employees can add a spouse and up to two children to the health plan at no extra cost. Parents can be added for a fee of $15 per month each.

## Employee development

Each employee has a learning budget of $800 per year for courses, certifications and conference tickets. Unused learning budget does not carry over to the next year.

Employees can apply for an internal transfer after 18 months in their current role. Internal transfers are handled by the people team and do not go through the external recruiting process.
