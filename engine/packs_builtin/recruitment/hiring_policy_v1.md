# Northwind Talent Co. Hiring Policy (Version 1)

Fictional demo document. Northwind Talent Co. is a made-up company used for testing.

This policy is effective from 1 January 2025. It applies to all permanent and fixed-term roles at Northwind Talent Co. offices in Pune and Jaipur.

## Approvals

Every new position must be approved by the hiring manager and the HR business partner before it is advertised. Positions with a salary above the top of the published band also need sign-off from the regional finance lead.

Approved positions stay open for 90 days. After 90 days the hiring manager must re-confirm the budget or close the position.

## Experience requirements

Entry-level roles do not have a minimum experience requirement. Mid-level roles require at least 2 years of relevant experience.

Senior roles, including the Senior Data Analyst role, require a minimum of 5 years of relevant experience. Relevant experience can include paid internships longer than 6 months.

## Employee referrals

Current employees may refer candidates for any open position. The referral bonus is $2,000 for each referred candidate who is hired and completes probation.

Referral bonuses are paid in the payroll cycle after the new hire passes probation. Employees in the recruiting team are not eligible for referral bonuses.

## Background checks

All finalists must consent to a background check before an offer is released. The background check must be completed within 10 business days of the candidate giving consent.

If a background check is not completed in time, the recruiter must inform the hiring manager and pause the offer.

## Offers

Offers are made in writing through the Northwind candidate portal. A written offer expires 7 calendar days after it is sent if the candidate has not accepted it.

Recruiters may extend an offer deadline once, by up to 3 calendar days, with approval from the hiring manager.

## Probation

New hires complete a probation period of 6 months. Probation can be extended once by up to 3 months if the manager documents a performance plan.
