# Northwind Talent Co. Recruiter Guide

Fictional demo document. Northwind Talent Co. is a made-up company used for testing.

This guide explains how recruiters screen candidates and run the interview process. It follows the current hiring policy.

## Screening steps

Recruiters must screen every new application within 3 business days of it being received. Screening has three steps: a CV review, a 20-minute phone screen and a check of salary expectations against the published band.

Candidates who pass screening must be told about the next stage within 2 business days of the phone screen.

## Candidate notice periods

Recruiters must confirm the candidate's current notice period during the phone screen. Candidates with a notice period longer than 60 days can only move forward with written approval from the hiring manager.

## Scorecards

Every interviewer must use the standard Northwind scorecard with a 1 to 4 rating for each competency. Interviewers must submit their scorecard within 24 hours of the interview.

Interviewers must not read other interviewers' scorecards before submitting their own. A candidate needs an average score of at least 3.0 across all scorecards to receive an offer.

## Questions recruiters must not ask

Recruiters must never ask candidates about their age, marital status, pregnancy or family plans, religion, or caste. Recruiters must not ask for a candidate's current salary slip, but they may ask for salary expectations.

Any recruiter who receives such information without asking must not record it in the candidate portal.

## Rejections and records

Rejected candidates must receive a written rejection within 5 business days of the decision. Candidate records are kept in the candidate portal for 12 months and then deleted.

Recruiters must not share interview feedback with candidates in writing. Verbal feedback may be given on request after the final stage.

## Candidate experience

Recruiters must reply to candidate emails within 1 business day. Interview slots must be offered at least 2 business days in advance, and candidates may reschedule an interview once without penalty.
