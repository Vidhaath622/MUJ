# Harbor Ridge Bank Credit Card Promotions and Rewards

Harbor Ridge Bank is a fictional bank used for demonstration only. This page covers the Voyager and Everyday Rewards credit cards. It does not cover checking, savings or debit card accounts. All amounts are in USD.

## Welcome Offers

New Voyager cardholders earn 40,000 bonus points after spending $3,000 in the first 90 days.

The welcome bonus is credited within 8 weeks after the spending requirement is met.

Customers who closed a Harbor Ridge credit card in the last 24 months are not eligible for the welcome bonus.

## Annual Fee

The Voyager card has an annual fee of $95, which is waived for the first year.

The Everyday Rewards card has no annual fee.

The annual fee is also waived for active-duty military members on request.

## International Use

The Voyager card charges no foreign transaction fee on international purchases.

The Everyday Rewards card charges a foreign transaction fee of 3% of each purchase made outside the United States.

Cash advances made at international ATMs with either card cost 5% of the amount or $10, whichever is greater.

## Introductory APR and Balance Transfers

The Everyday Rewards card offers a 0% introductory APR on purchases for 15 months from account opening.

Balance transfers made within 60 days of account opening carry a fee of 3% of the transferred amount.

After the introductory period, the variable purchase APR is between 19.99% and 27.99% depending on creditworthiness.

## Rewards and Credits

Cardholders earn 2 points per dollar on travel and dining and 1 point per dollar on everything else.

Points expire if the card account has no purchases for 18 months.

A statement credit for a promotional charge that was billed in error is posted within 2 billing cycles after the cardholder contacts the bank.

## Late and Returned Payments

A late payment fee of up to $29 applies if the minimum payment is not received by the due date.

A returned payment fee of up to $29 applies to a payment that is returned unpaid.

The late fee is waived once per 12 months for cardholders enrolled in autopay.
