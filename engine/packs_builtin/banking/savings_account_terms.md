# Harbor Ridge Bank Savings Account Terms

These terms apply to the Harbor Ridge Bank Everyday Savings account. Harbor Ridge Bank is a fictional bank used for demonstration only. All amounts are in USD.

## Opening and Minimum Balance

The minimum deposit to open an Everyday Savings account is $50.

A monthly service fee of $5 applies to every Everyday Savings account.

The $5 monthly service fee is waived when the account keeps a minimum daily balance of $300 or when the customer sets up an automatic monthly transfer of at least $25 from a Harbor Ridge checking account.

## Interest Tiers

Interest is calculated daily on the full collected balance and credited monthly on the last business day of the month.

The account pays an annual percentage yield of 0.50% on balances below $10,000.

Balances from $10,000 to $49,999.99 earn an annual percentage yield of 1.25%.

Balances of $50,000 or more earn an annual percentage yield of 2.10%.

Rates are variable and may change at any time after the account is opened. The tier that applies is set by the end-of-day balance.

## Withdrawal Limits

Customers may make up to 6 withdrawals or outgoing transfers from the account per statement cycle.

Each withdrawal beyond the sixth in a statement cycle incurs an excess withdrawal fee of $10.

If excess withdrawals happen in three statement cycles within any 12-month period, the bank may convert the account to a checking account.

Withdrawals made in person at a branch or at a Harbor Ridge Bank ATM do not count toward the limit.

## Dormant Accounts

An account becomes dormant when there is no customer-initiated activity for 12 consecutive months.

A dormant account is charged a dormancy fee of $3 per month, but the fee never reduces the balance below zero.

Interest continues to accrue on dormant accounts at the applicable tier rate.

To reactivate a dormant account, the customer must visit a branch with valid photo identification or call the service center and pass identity verification.

If an account stays dormant for 3 years, the balance is reported and transferred to the state as unclaimed property, as required by law.

## Closing the Account

If the account is closed within 90 days of opening, an early closure fee of $25 applies. Accrued interest that has not yet been credited is forfeited when the account is closed before the end of a month.
