# Harbor Ridge Bank Consumer Fee Schedule (Version 2)

Effective date: July 1, 2026. Harbor Ridge Bank is a fictional bank used for demonstration only. This schedule applies to personal checking accounts. All amounts are in USD.

This version supersedes the Consumer Fee Schedule (Version 1) dated January 1, 2025. Where the two versions differ, this version controls.

## Monthly Maintenance

The Everyday Checking account carries a monthly maintenance fee of $12.

The monthly maintenance fee is waived when the account keeps a minimum daily balance of $1,000 for the entire statement cycle.

The fee is also waived for account holders under age 24 and for accounts that receive at least one direct deposit of $500 or more each statement cycle.

## Overdraft and Returned Items

The overdraft fee is $25 per item when the bank pays a transaction that overdraws the account.

The bank charges at most 3 overdraft fees per business day. No overdraft fee is charged if the account is overdrawn by $10 or less at the end of the business day.

A returned item fee of $35 applies when the bank declines to pay a check or electronic payment for insufficient funds.

## Wire Transfers

An incoming domestic wire transfer is free. An outgoing domestic wire transfer costs $25.

An outgoing international wire transfer costs $40 per transfer when sent through online banking and $50 per transfer when sent through a branch.

Incoming international wires carry a $15 receiving fee. Intermediary banks may deduct their own charges before funds arrive.

## ATM Use

Withdrawals at Harbor Ridge Bank ATMs are free.

Each withdrawal at an out-of-network ATM costs $2.50, in addition to any fee charged by the ATM owner.

ATM withdrawals made outside the United States carry a fee of 3% of the withdrawal amount.

## Other Services

A stop payment order costs $30 per request and stays active for 6 months.

A cashier's check costs $10. Paper statements cost $3 per month; electronic statements are free.

A replacement debit card is free for the first request each calendar year and $5 for each additional request. Expedited card delivery costs $20.

Account research requested by the customer is billed at $25 per hour, with a minimum charge of one hour.

## Changes to This Schedule

The bank will give at least 30 days' written notice before increasing any fee in this schedule. Fee decreases may take effect without notice.
