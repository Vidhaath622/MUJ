# Internal Procedure: Debit Card and Electronic Transaction Disputes

INTERNAL USE ONLY. Harbor Ridge Bank (fictional, demonstration only). Procedure owner: Deposit Operations. This procedure covers disputes on checking and savings accounts, including debit card and electronic transfers.

## Filing Deadlines

Customers must report an unauthorized debit card or electronic transaction within 60 calendar days after the statement date on which the transaction first appeared.

Disputes reported after the 60-day window are reviewed case by case and are not eligible for provisional credit.

A lost or stolen debit card reported within 2 business days limits the customer's liability to $50.

## Documents Required

Staff must collect a signed Dispute Affidavit (form DR-12), a copy of the relevant account statement, and any receipts or correspondence with the merchant.

For card-not-present fraud, staff must also record the last date the customer had physical possession of the card.

Cases with missing documents stay in Pending Documents status for up to 10 business days before they are closed.

## Provisional Credit

If the investigation cannot be completed within 10 business days of receiving the dispute, the bank must issue provisional credit for the disputed amount.

Provisional credit is posted to the account within 10 business days of receiving the dispute and the customer is notified in writing within 2 business days of posting.

For accounts open less than 30 days, provisional credit may be delayed up to 20 business days.

## Investigation Timeline

The investigation must be completed within 45 calendar days of receiving the dispute.

For transactions made outside the United States or at point-of-sale terminals, the investigation period may be extended to 90 calendar days.

If the dispute is resolved against the customer, provisional credit is reversed with 5 business days' written notice.

## Escalation Steps

Step 1: The branch or call center agent logs the case in the dispute system on the same business day.

Step 2: If a case is not resolved within 30 calendar days, it is escalated to the Dispute Review Team.

Step 3: Disputed amounts above $5,000 must be approved by a Deposit Operations manager before any final decision.

Step 4: Suspected internal fraud is referred to the Financial Crimes Unit within 1 business day and is not discussed with the customer.

## Closing a Case

Every closed case must include a written resolution letter sent to the customer. Records are kept for 5 years after the case is closed.
