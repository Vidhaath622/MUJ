# GST Rate Schedule (Prior to September 2025 Rationalisation)

Under the standard Goods and Services Tax (GST) rate structure established in India, goods and services were broadly categorized into four primary tax slabs: 5%, 12%, 18%, and 28%.

## Key Rates for Selected Common Items (Pre-Reform)
- Air Conditioners and TVs (>32 inches): Taxed at the peak rate of 28%.
- Cement and Construction Materials: Taxed at the highest rate slab of 28%.
- Medicines and Medical Devices: Standard essential drugs, medical equipment, and diagnostic kits were taxed at 12%.
- Spectacles and Corrective Lenses: Spectacles, contact lenses, and corrective frames carried a 12% GST rate.
- Hotel Room Accommodation: Hotel room stays priced up to ₹7,500 per day were taxed at 12%.
- Gyms and Salons: Fitness centers, beauty salons, and barbershops were subject to 18% GST.
- Packaged Processed Foods: Packaged namkeens, pasta, sauces, and biscuits were taxed at 12% or 18%.
- Small Automobiles & Two-Wheelers: Two-wheelers and small engine motor vehicles were taxed at 28%.
- Bicycles and Toothpaste: Daily household articles like bicycles and toothpaste were taxed at 12%.
