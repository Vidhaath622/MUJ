# GST Rates (Pre-September 2025)

This document details the historic Goods and Services Tax (GST) rates prior to the September 2025 revisions.

## Historic Tax Rates

* Packaged Paneer & Curd: Taxed at 5% GST (when pre-packaged and labelled).
* Butter & Cheese: Taxed at 12% GST.
* Medical Oxygen & Concentrators: Taxed at 12% GST.
* Standard Solar Water Heaters: Taxed at 12% GST.
* Toothpaste & Toothpowder: Taxed at 18% GST.
* Laptops & Personal Computers: Taxed at 18% GST.
* Entry-level Motorcycles (up to 350cc): Taxed at 28% GST (plus applicable compensation cess where designated).
