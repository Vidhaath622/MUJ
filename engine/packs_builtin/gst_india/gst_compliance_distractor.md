# GST Compliance Guidelines & Deadlines

This document outlines key procedural compliance requirements for registered taxpayers under the GST framework.

## Return Filing Deadlines & Rules

* GSTR-1 (Outward Supplies): Monthly GSTR-1 returns must be filed by the 11th of every succeeding month for regular taxpayers.
* GSTR-3B (Summary Return): Monthly summary return GSTR-3B along with self-assessed tax payment is due by the 20th of the following month.

## E-Invoicing Regulations

* Applicability Threshold: E-invoicing is mandatory for all registered businesses with an aggregate turnover exceeding ₹5 Crore in any preceding financial year.
