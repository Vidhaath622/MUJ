# GST Rates (Post-September 2025 Revision)

This document contains the revised Goods and Services Tax (GST) rates applicable following the September 2025 rationalisation process.

## Revised Tax Rates

* Packaged Paneer & Curd: Exempted (0% GST) to encourage nutritional food affordability.
* Butter & Cheese: Reduced to 5% GST under the essential dairy rationalisation umbrella.
* Medical Oxygen & Concentrators: Reduced to 5% GST under essential life-support healthcare provisions.
* Laptops & Personal Computers: Reduced to 12% GST to foster educational technology adoption.
* Entry-level Motorcycles (up to 350cc): Reduced to 18% GST to promote mass commuter mobility.
* Standard Solar Water Heaters: Increased to 18% GST under unified green energy hardware alignment.
