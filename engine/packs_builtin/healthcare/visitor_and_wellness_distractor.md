# Mercy Vale General Hospital: Visitor Amenities and Wellness Guide

Fictional document for software testing. Not medical guidance.

Document owner: Facilities and Patient Experience Team. Effective date: 1 January 2026.

## Welcome to visitors

Visitors are an important part of every patient's stay. This guide explains the amenities available to visitors, the hospital's wellness programmes and how to find your way around.

## Visitor Lounge

The Visitor Lounge on the first floor is open from 07:00 to 21:00 every day. It has free tea and coffee, phone charging points and a quiet room for visitors who need a break.

Visitors waiting for long periods may borrow a pager from the lounge desk so that they can step out and be called back.

## Cafeteria and shops

The main cafeteria is open from 07:30 to 19:30 on weekdays and from 09:00 to 17:00 at weekends. Hot meals are served between 12:00 and 14:00 and between 17:00 and 19:00.

The ground floor shop sells newspapers, snacks and flowers, and is open seven days a week. Flowers are not permitted on some wards, so visitors should check with the ward first.

## Parking

Visitor parking is available in the multi-storey car park next to the main entrance. The first 30 minutes are free. Parking permit requests for long-stay visitors are processed within 3 working days by the Facilities Office.

Blue badge holders can park free of charge in the marked bays close to the main entrance.

## Wellness programmes

The hospital runs free wellness programmes for visitors, carers and staff. Programmes include a weekly mindful walking group, a carers' support circle and a healthy eating course.

Wellness follow-up sessions are scheduled 14 days after each course ends, so participants can talk about their progress. Follow-up sessions last one hour and are held in the Wellness Room on the second floor.

## Hand-washing workshops

The Patient Experience Team holds a public hand-washing workshop for visitors once a month. The workshop shows visitors how to use the hand gel stations placed at every ward entrance.

## Hours of the chaplaincy and quiet spaces

The multi-faith chaplaincy is open 24 hours a day. A chaplain is on site from 09:00 to 17:00 on weekdays and on call at other times.

## Feedback

Visitors can leave feedback at the Patient Experience desk or by using the comment cards in the Visitor Lounge. The team replies to written feedback within 10 days.
