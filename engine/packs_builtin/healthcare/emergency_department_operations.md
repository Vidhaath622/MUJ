# Mercy Vale General Hospital: Emergency Department Operations

Fictional document for software testing. Not medical guidance.

Document owner: Emergency Department Operations Lead. Effective date: 1 January 2026.

## Triage levels and target wait times

Every patient arriving at the Emergency Department is assigned a triage level from 1 to 5 by the triage nurse within 15 minutes of registration. The levels below are operational targets for time to first assessment, used for reporting.

Level 1 patients are seen immediately, with no waiting time. Level 2 patients have a target wait of 10 minutes. Level 3 patients have a target wait of 60 minutes. Level 4 patients have a target wait of 2 hours. Level 5 patients have a target wait of 4 hours.

The department reports performance against these targets to the Operations Board every month.

## Time in department

The total time from arrival to admission, transfer or discharge should not exceed 4 hours for at least 95 percent of patients.

When any patient has been in the department for 6 hours, the Nurse in Charge must notify the Site Duty Manager. When any patient has been in the department for 12 hours, the Site Duty Manager must inform the on-call Executive Director.

## Escalation levels

The department uses three escalation levels based on occupancy. Escalation Level A applies when occupancy exceeds 85 percent of cubicles. Escalation Level B applies when occupancy exceeds 100 percent or more than 8 patients are waiting for a ward bed. Escalation Level C applies when ambulance handovers are delayed by more than 30 minutes for 3 or more ambulances at once.

At Level B or above, the Site Duty Manager holds a bed meeting every 2 hours.

## Staffing ratios

The department is staffed with 1 registered nurse for every 4 majors cubicles. The resuscitation area is staffed with 1 registered nurse for every 2 resuscitation bays. The minor injuries stream has at least 2 emergency nurse practitioners on duty between 08:00 and 22:00.

A consultant is physically present in the department from 08:00 to midnight, seven days a week.

## Companions in the department

Companions may accompany patients in the Emergency Department at any hour, limited to one companion per patient. Children under 16 attending as patients may have two parents or guardians with them.

## Infection control audits

Hand-hygiene audits in the Emergency Department are carried out every 2 weeks by the infection prevention link nurse. Results are displayed on the staff noticeboard and shared at the monthly governance meeting.

Any audit score below 90 percent triggers a repeat audit within 7 days.
