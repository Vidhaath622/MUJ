# Mercy Vale General Hospital: Patient Records Access and Retention Policy

Fictional document for software testing. Not medical guidance.

Document owner: Health Records Manager. Effective date: 1 April 2026.

## Who can request records

Patients may request copies of their own health records. A parent or guardian may request records for a child under 16. A person holding a registered power of attorney may request records on the patient's behalf.

Requests from solicitors or insurers must include signed written consent from the patient.

## How to submit a request

Requests are submitted on the Records Access Form, available from the main reception desk or the hospital website. Completed forms can be posted to the Health Records Department or handed in at the main reception desk.

## Identity verification

The Health Records Department verifies identity before releasing any record. The requester must provide two forms of identification, one of which must be photographic and one of which must show their current address. Proof of address must be dated within the last 3 months.

If the requester is acting for a patient, they must also provide the document that gives them authority, such as a power of attorney.

## Processing times

Requests for copies of health records are processed within 15 working days of receiving a complete request with verified identity. Complex requests covering more than 5 years of records may be extended by a further 20 working days, and the requester is told about the extension in writing.

The processing clock does not start until identity verification is complete.

## Fees

Electronic copies of records are provided free of charge. Paper copies cost a flat fee of 25 credits per request to cover printing and postage. Copies of imaging on disc cost 15 credits per disc.

Fees are waived for patients receiving income support.

## Retention periods

Adult inpatient records are retained for 8 years after the last episode of care. Records of children are retained until the patient's 25th birthday, or for 8 years after the last episode of care if that is later. Maternity records are retained for 25 years.

Records due for destruction are reviewed by the Health Records Manager every quarter.

## Corrections and complaints

Patients who believe their record contains an error can ask for a correction note to be added. The Health Records Manager responds to correction requests within 30 days.
