# Mercy Vale General Hospital: Patient Discharge Protocol (Version 1, 2025)

Fictional document for software testing. Not medical guidance.

Document owner: Patient Flow Office. Effective date: 1 March 2025. Review cycle: annual.

## Purpose and scope

This protocol sets out the administrative steps for discharging adult inpatients from general wards at Mercy Vale General Hospital. It covers paperwork, communication with the family doctor, visitor arrangements on the day of discharge and follow-up contact. It does not cover clinical decisions about whether a patient is ready to leave.

## Planned discharge time

Ward teams should aim to complete planned discharges by 11:00 on the day of discharge. Patients who are ready to leave but are waiting for transport move to the Discharge Lounge on the ground floor, which is staffed from 08:00 to 20:00 on weekdays.

The ward clerk confirms the expected discharge date with the patient at least 24 hours in advance.

## Discharge paperwork

Every patient receives a printed discharge letter before leaving the ward. The letter lists the admitting consultant, the ward name, the date of admission and the date of discharge.

The discharge summary is sent to the patient's family doctor within 5 business days of discharge. The ward clerk records the date the summary was sent in the Patient Administration System.

Missing paperwork is escalated to the Ward Manager, who must resolve it before the end of the same shift.

## Medicines reconciliation

A pharmacy review is required before discharge for any patient going home with 5 or more regular medications. The review is booked through the ward pharmacy request form and is normally completed within 4 hours of the request.

Patients with fewer medications receive a standard medicines list printed with the discharge letter.

## Visitors on the day of discharge

A maximum of 4 visitors may be present at the bedside while discharge paperwork is being completed. Visitors helping with transport should wait in the Discharge Lounge if the ward is busy.

## Follow-up contact

A nurse from the Patient Flow Office makes a follow-up phone call within 72 hours of discharge. The call checks that the patient received their paperwork and understands who to contact with administrative questions.

If the patient cannot be reached, the nurse tries again the next working day and then sends a letter.

A follow-up outpatient appointment, where one is needed, is booked within 10 working days of discharge.

## Escalation contacts

Administrative problems with a discharge are escalated in this order: Ward Clerk, Ward Manager, Patient Flow Coordinator, Site Duty Manager. The Site Duty Manager is available 24 hours a day on extension 4400.
