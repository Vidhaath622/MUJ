# Income Tax Slabs for New Tax Regime (Financial Year 2024–25 / Assessment Year 2025–26)

Under Section 115BAC of the Income Tax Act, 1961, the revised new tax regime slabs for FY 2024–25 apply as follows:

- Income up to ₹300,000: Nil
- Income from ₹300,001 to ₹600,000: 5%
- Income from ₹600,001 to ₹900,000: 10%
- Income from ₹900,001 to ₹1,200,000: 15%
- Income from ₹1,200,001 to ₹1,500,000: 20%
- Income above ₹1,500,000: 30%

## Key Benefits and Deductions
- Rebate under Section 87A: Taxpayers with taxable income up to ₹700,000 pay zero income tax due to a full rebate.
- Standard Deduction: A standard deduction of ₹75,000 is available for salaried individuals and pensioners under the new tax regime.
- Salaried Tax-Free Threshold: Salaried employees pay no income tax up to ₹775,000 after accounting for the standard deduction.
- Surcharge Rates: Surcharge under the new regime is capped at 25% for taxable income exceeding ₹2,000,000.
