# Income Tax Slabs for New Tax Regime (Financial Year 2025–26 / Assessment Year 2026–27)

Following Union Budget 2025 proposals, the updated income tax rate structure under the new regime for FY 2025–26 is:

- Income up to ₹400,000: Nil
- Income from ₹400,001 to ₹800,000: 5%
- Income from ₹800,001 to ₹1,200,000: 10%
- Income from ₹1,200,001 to ₹1,600,000: 15%
- Income from ₹1,600,001 to ₹2,000,000: 20%
- Income from ₹2,000,001 to ₹2,400,000: 25%
- Income above ₹2,400,000: 30%

## Key Benefits and Deductions
- Rebate under Section 87A: Taxpayers with taxable income up to ₹1,200,000 pay zero tax due to a full tax rebate.
- Standard Deduction: A standard deduction of ₹75,000 is available for salaried individuals and pensioners under the new tax regime.
- Salaried Tax-Free Threshold: Salaried employees pay no income tax up to ₹1,275,000 after accounting for the standard deduction.
- Health and Education Cess: Health and Education Cess remains at 4% on the total income tax amount.
