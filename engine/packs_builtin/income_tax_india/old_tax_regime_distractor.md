# Old Tax Regime Provisions and Exemptions (Financial Year 2024–25 & FY 2025–26)

The Old Tax Regime continues to coexist with the New Tax Regime as an optional taxation framework.

## Tax Slabs (Individual Taxpayers below 60 years)
- Income up to ₹250,000: Nil
- Income from ₹250,001 to ₹500,000: 5%
- Income from ₹500,001 to ₹1,000,000: 20%
- Income above ₹1,000,000: 30%

## Common Deductions Available
- Section 80C: Deduction up to ₹150,000 for EPF, PPF, ELSS, tuition fees, and principal repayment on home loans.
- Section 80D: Deduction up to ₹25,000 for health insurance premiums paid for self and family (₹50,000 for senior citizens).
- House Rent Allowance (HRA): Exemption available under Section 10(13A).
