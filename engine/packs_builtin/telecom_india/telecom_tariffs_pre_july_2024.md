# Indian Telecom Standard Prepaid Tariff Schedule (Pre-July 2024)

Prior to the industry-wide tariff adjustments in July 2024, standard prepaid plans for primary telecom operators (Jio and Airtel) were priced as follows:

- 28-Day Base 1.5GB/day Plan: Priced at ₹239, providing unlimited voice calling and 1.5GB daily high-speed data.
- 84-Day Popular 1.5GB/day Plan: Priced at ₹666, providing 84 days validity with 1.5GB daily data allotment.
- 84-Day 2GB/day Plan: Priced at ₹719, offering 84 days validity with 2GB daily data.
- 365-Day Annual Plan: Priced at ₹2,999, providing 365 days validity with 2.5GB daily data.
- 5G Data Eligibility: Unlimited 5G data access was included free on all plans offering 1.5GB/day or higher.
- Data Top-up Booster (1GB): 1GB high-speed data add-on pack was priced at ₹15.
