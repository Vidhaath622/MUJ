# Indian Telecom Standard Prepaid Tariff Schedule (Post-July 2024)

Effective July 2024, major telecom service providers implemented nationwide tariff increases across prepaid product categories:

- 28-Day Base 1.5GB/day Plan: Price increased to ₹299, providing unlimited voice calling and 1.5GB daily high-speed data.
- 84-Day Popular 1.5GB/day Plan: Price increased to ₹799, providing 84 days validity with 1.5GB daily data allotment.
- 84-Day 2GB/day Plan: Price increased to ₹859, offering 84 days validity with 2GB daily data.
- 365-Day Annual Plan: Price increased to ₹3,599, providing 365 days validity with 2.5GB daily data.
- 5G Data Eligibility: Unlimited 5G data access is now restricted exclusively to plans offering 2GB/day or higher.
- Data Top-up Booster (1GB): 1GB high-speed data add-on pack price increased to ₹19.
