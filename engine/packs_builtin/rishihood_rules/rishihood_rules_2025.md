# Rishihood University (Sonipat) Academic Regulations (2025–26 Session)

## Attendance Policy & Evaluation Rules (2025 Revision)
- Minimum Attendance Requirement: Students must maintain a minimum of 80% attendance in each course to be eligible to sit for the end-term examinations.
- Attendance Condonation Threshold: Shortfall between 70% and 79% may be condoned by the Dean on valid medical grounds upon submitting medical certificates.
- Causal Leave Allowance: Students are permitted a maximum of 8 days of casual leave per semester for official university extra-curricular activities.
- End-Term Examination Weightage: The continuous evaluation scheme assigns 60% weightage to continuous assessments and mid-terms, and 40% weightage to end-term final examinations.
- Grade Improvement Exam Fee: Re-examination fee for grade improvement is ₹1,500 per paper.
